- Date: 2026-10-18

- Scope: Studio Manual interpreter in the approved test campaign only
- Protected boundary: Scene acceptance, pending-binding records and receipts

Adopt the governing decision's complete problem, terminology, authority and state
lifetimes, versioned schemas, data/prompt flow, unchanged budgets, validation and
repair behavior, player recovery, compatibility/rollback, observability, examples,
implementation plan and acceptance gates. Runtime work is subordinate to those
accepted decisions.

GMC owns one canonical deterministic compiler. It resolves an exact prepared
affordance selection to that row's coverage-entry identity, and derives only the
redundant receiving-controller coverage from its exact already-selected sensory
group and verified current owner relationship. It cannot change world content,
semantic targets, methods, access, effects or outcomes. Generated GMA code shares
the implementation; independent semantic examination still gates acceptance.

The supported deterministic compilation endpoint rereads owner context and does
not write campaign state or call a model. The existing protected Scene save must
reconstruct original choices, independently recompile from current owner bodies,
compare the complete closed proof and require assessment `/8`. New pending-binding
record and intent-binding receipt `/6` retain canonical binding `/5` plus the
exact compilation/review identity. No client fingerprint alone establishes
authority or authenticates external-LLM authorship.

Historical construction/checkpoints/validation outcomes remain immutable under
their original profiles. An explicit compatibility compiler may consume an
unedited profile-14 final reply before its not-yet-issued new independent review;
it must not relabel the old validation, reset the original ledger or add an AI
operation. Already issued older reviews remain on their original contract.

The receiver may be a grammatical actor or explicit recipient, but only an exact
verified parent link permits derivation. New review-only policy removes inherited
builder formatting instructions without removing semantic checks. Its profitable
lossless sharing retains all decoded evidence and the original 131,072-byte
registered input limit; historical operation codecs remain unchanged.

Reader-first delivery precedes GMA emission and exact Studio pins. Full checks,
fresh independent browser proof and exact Ready deployments precede original
live recovery. No provider activation, larger byte/call allowance, new gameplay
writer, completed-action replay, global rollout or VCS change is authorized.
