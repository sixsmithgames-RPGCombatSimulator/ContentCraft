# Scene presented-target closure correction

Governing decisions: Accepted ADR-006 and GMA ADR-011. This correction implements
their existing exact actor identity and presented-threshold closure; it does not
introduce a new authority, schema, prompt policy, rollout, or rebase design.

## Evidence and implementation plan

A blind Manual investigative build prepared a familiar in an actor frame with
its exact `actorRef`, and a tunnel mouth in a zone's threshold record with its
exact `boundaryId`. The original builder policy already requires every
materially addressable actor and threshold to cite an exact prepared ref.
The registered semantic validator catalog omitted both identity forms. The
owner opening-frame validator also omitted the actor alias and zone-local
thresholds. The unchanged proposal was rejected before examination or writes.

1. Derive one shared, pure presented-target reference catalog from the submitted
   dossier's exact zone, actor-frame/actor, element, fact, prepared-boundary and
   zone-threshold IDs. Do not include display names or arbitrary source refs.
2. Use it at both Manual semantic validation and owner opening-frame validation.
   Keep all other shape, tenant, source, assessment, CAS and authority checks.
3. Test both exact actor identity forms, root and zone-local thresholds, removed
   preparation, forged source-only refs and labels, plus original policy inclusion.
4. Replay the unedited blind capture through GMA and GMC, stopping at the next
   genuine boundary. Run full owner, assistant and exact Studio checks and
   compiled/serverless import checks before direct-main delivery.

## Impact, risk and limits

Only opening-manifest reference closure changes. A matching dossier identity is
not permission, presence, reveal authority, a certificate or a mechanics result.
The principal risk is accepting unprepared references; negative tests and the
unchanged owner validations protect that boundary. No live campaign, Gemini
provider or VCS change is authorized by this test. Keep fresh intake disabled.
Rollback restores the prior validator while retaining captured replies and
existing saved data; no migration or replay is required. Remaining certification,
execution, narration, browser and human-GM gates must be reported separately.
